Rink Music

A working copy of the iPhone app. The buttons, timers, fades and timeline editor follow the same rules as the Swift code, so you can try the game-day flow before installing anything.

  • WarmupTap Start Warmup; the red Stop at the top of every section stops any music that's playing. The songs play back to back and fade out when the 3:00 clock runs out. Drop the length to 0:30 with the stepper to see the ending quickly.
  • StingerTap a clip to play it, tap it again to fade it. Use FADE or STOP in the bottom bar.
  • GoalTap Play Random Goal, or pick a specific clip.
  • OrderDrag a clip by its ☰ handle to move it up or down the list. With a mouse you can also drag the whole row. The warmup plays in this order.
  • EditingTap the slider icon on any clip. Drag the white handles to trim, tap the waveform to move the playhead, split, and set fades and volume. Changes are audible while the preview plays.

The demo clips are generated tones and drum patterns. Tap + in the app to try your own MP3 or M4A files. Nothing is saved here: reloading the page starts over. In the real app, swipe a clip left to delete or duplicate it; here those buttons are at the bottom of the editor.
